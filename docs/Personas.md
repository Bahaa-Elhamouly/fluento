# Personas

## Student

Primary MVP user.

Goals:

- Learn German, Spanish, or French.
- Follow a structured path.
- Practice vocabulary, grammar, listening, reading, writing, and speaking.
- Receive instant feedback.
- Track progress and build habits.
- Use AI for conversation and explanation.

Pain points:

- Losing consistency.
- Not knowing what to practice next.
- Passive learning without speaking or writing.
- Lack of feedback.

## Admin

Primary internal platform operator.

Goals:

- Manage users and content.
- Maintain course quality.
- Monitor payments, analytics, and notifications.
- Configure AI behavior and learning content.
- Review reports and operational health.

Pain points:

- Manual content updates.
- No visibility into learner progress or platform errors.
- Inconsistent quiz and lesson quality.

## Future Teacher

Future role.

Goals:

- Assign lessons.
- Review student progress.
- Manage classroom cohorts.

## Future Organization Buyer

Future role.

Goals:

- Purchase language learning for teams.
- Monitor adoption and outcomes.
- Manage users, seats, and reports.
