# Fluento Vision

Fluento exists to help learners build practical fluency across languages through structured lessons, active recall, guided practice, and AI-assisted feedback.

## Long-Term Direction

Fluento will become a multilingual learning ecosystem that supports:

- Self-paced learning paths.
- Vocabulary and grammar mastery.
- Speaking, listening, reading, and writing practice.
- AI tutor and conversation partner experiences.
- Personalized learning plans.
- Progress, achievements, goals, and gamification.
- Premium subscriptions.
- Admin management and analytics.
- Future teacher, school, company, organization, and enterprise workflows.

## Initial Languages

- German.
- Spanish.
- French.

Language support must be extensible through content and configuration, not hardcoded assumptions.

## Product Positioning

Fluento is not only a flashcard app, grammar reference, chatbot, or course catalog. It combines these into a guided learning system where progress is measurable and practice is adaptive.
